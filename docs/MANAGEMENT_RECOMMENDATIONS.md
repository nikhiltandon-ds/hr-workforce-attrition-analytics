# Management Recommendations

These are portfolio recommendations based on descriptive patterns in the dataset.

- Focus retention reviews on segments with both elevated attrition rates and sufficient headcount exposure.
- Strengthen first-90-day and first-year onboarding/check-in routines for early-tenure employees.
- Monitor overtime as an early workforce-health indicator and review workload allocation where overtime-linked attrition is elevated.
- Use salary bands to identify areas for compensation benchmarking; do not infer that pay alone causes attrition.
- Review manager span, career progression and role clarity in high-attrition job roles.
- Use dashboard trends over repeated reporting periods to evaluate whether interventions coincide with improvement.
