# Data Dictionary

- EmployeeNumber: employee identifier.
- Attrition: Yes/No employee exit indicator.
- Department: business department.
- JobRole: employee role.
- JobLevel: internal role level from 1 to 5.
- MonthlyIncome: monthly income measure in dataset units.
- YearsAtCompany: tenure in years.
- TotalWorkingYears: total working experience in years.
- OverTime: Yes/No overtime indicator.
- DistanceFromHome: commute-distance measure.
- JobSatisfaction: ordinal satisfaction score from 1 to 4.
- EnvironmentSatisfaction: ordinal score from 1 to 4.
- WorkLifeBalance: ordinal score from 1 to 4.
- SalaryBand: derived compensation band.
- TenureBand: derived tenure band.
- AgeBand: derived age band.
